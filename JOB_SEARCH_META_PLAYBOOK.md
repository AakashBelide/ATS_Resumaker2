# Getting Interviews in 2026 — The Meta-Game Beyond the Resume

> Companion to `RESUME_SYSTEM_BLUEPRINT.md`. The blueprint covers the **artifact** (resume/ATS/sponsorship data/tooling). This doc covers the **behavior** — *when/where/how fast/through whom* you apply, what proof-of-competence actually gets interviews per role, and the F1/OPT-specific game. Target roles: AI Engineer, Data Engineer, Data Scientist, SWE-AI, Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). Perspective: F1/OPT international student, US market, August 2026.
>
> **Every claim below is tagged for source integrity.** This entire topic is saturated with vendors selling the exact urgency they "research." Ratings: **WORKING / NOT WORKING / MIXED / UNVERIFIED / SCAM**, plus **genuine** (real practitioner/insider/attorney) vs **selling** (product/course/coaching funnel). Based on ~130 searches across Blind, Reddit, Hacker News, Trustpilot, immigration-attorney firms, DOJ/ICE/USCIS, and university international offices — down-weighting vendor blogs and astroturfed reviews.

---

## 0. The five things that actually move the needle (if you read nothing else)

1. **Referrals beat every timing/tool trick — but for you they only count at confirmed-sponsor companies.** Referrals get you *seen* (multiple genuine funnels show 4–10× the interview rate of cold apply), but a referral **cannot override a no-sponsorship policy** — people report rejection at HR screen or even *after final rounds* once status is disclosed. So aim 100% of your networking energy at companies with a proven H-1B track record. *(Genuine — Blind; high confidence.)*
2. **The 2026 wage-weighted H-1B lottery is the biggest new headwind.** Effective Feb 27, 2026: Level-4 wage = 4 lottery entries, Level-1 = 1. Attorney estimates put entry-level (Level 1) selection odds roughly **halving (~30% → ~15%)**. Counter it by targeting **higher wage tiers**, **big-immigration-dept employers**, and **cap-exempt** research orgs (no lottery). *(Genuine — Ogletree/Mintz/DHS; high confidence.)*
3. **"Apply within the first hour" is largely a myth sold by alert-tool vendors.** The real, mechanism-backed effect is *don't apply to stale postings* — skip anything >1 month old. Apply Day 0–1 as cheap insurance, don't lose sleep over the exact clock. *(Vendor stats UNVERIFIED; freshness heuristic genuine.)*
4. **A deployed project that leads with an evaluation harness is the strongest non-resume signal for AI/ML/data roles** — far more than certs, Kaggle notebooks, or ChatGPT-wrapper demos (all oversaturated). *(Genuine — DataTalks/Alexey Grigorev field guide; high confidence.)*
5. **The whole tools/services ecosystem is mostly noise.** The only clearly-worth-it items are free: GitHub new-grad lists, Hiring.cafe, Simplify autofill, and the H-1B sponsorship-data tools. Auto-*submit* bots and reverse recruiters are waste-of-money at best, status-endangering at worst. *(Genuine — Trustpilot/Blind/HN; high confidence.)*

## 1. Application timing & velocity

### The "apply early" question — mostly vendor myth
- **"90% of interviews go to first-24h applicants" / "72% of offers to first-5-days"** → trace to EvalCommunity and a "GoApply study" surfaced almost only on jobstrack.io, **which sells career-page alerts.** No methodology. **UNVERIFIED / selling.**
- **"Apply 6–10am = 5× interviews"** → recycled ~2010s Payscale data, not 2026. **Debunked.**
- **"Apply Day 4 not Day 1 for 61% response"** → one resume-service influencer's unverifiable 347-app dataset. **Debunked.**
- **Genuine consensus (Blind, no product):** time of day is irrelevant; the real effect is *avoiding stale postings*. Roles now hit 200+ applicants Day 1, 500–1000 in 48h; recruiters often shortlist from the first ~25–200 resumes. So Day 0–1 is a **cheap hedge**, not a decisive lever. A referral dwarfs it.
- **Verdict:** apply fast to *fresh* postings, ignore the "first hour" hype.

### Freshness pipeline (WORKING, free, no ban risk)
- **GitHub community lists** — `SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions`, `SimplifyJobs/Summer-Internships`, `vanshb03/New-Grad-2027`, `speedyapply/2027-SWE-College-Jobs`. Daily-updated, PR-audited (ghost jobs get flagged fast), "posted date" columns. *(Genuine, FREE-AND-GOOD.)* Caveat: US lists, no visa tagging — cross-reference sponsorship data.
- **Hiring.cafe** — indexes **directly from company ATS/career pages** (Greenhouse/Ashby/Lever), so fresher and lower-competition than LinkedIn Easy Apply, with visa/remote filters. Community favorite. *(Genuine, FREE-AND-GOOD.)*
- **Principle:** aggregators lag the company source 12–72h. Watch the source. Set LinkedIn saved-search alerts + check target companies' own career pages directly.

### Volume vs quality
- Cold/generic app→interview ≈ **1–3%**; tailored ≈ 3–5%; ~42 apps per interview as a rough baseline. New grads report **80–150 apps** as "normal" for a few interviews. *(Mixed survey + anecdote; directional.)*
- **OPT reality:** cited ~**44.6% of OPT holders** land jobs post-grad vs ~62% of domestic peers — expect materially lower conversion, so you need **targeted volume**: hundreds of apps, but concentrated on sponsor-friendly, fresh, genuinely-matched roles. Pure spray-and-pray = **NOT WORKING** (recruiters filter harder now that AI pushed LinkedIn apps +45% YoY).

### Ghost jobs (~1 in 5 postings)
- Greenhouse: ~18–22% of postings are ghosts; Clarify Capital: 1 in 3 employers admits posting no-intent roles. *(Employer/platform surveys — directional.)*
- **Detection (genuine, free):** skip roles live >1 month, roles absent from the company's own career page, and titles reposting every few weeks. Freshness correlates with realness — another reason to favor Day-0/1 fresh postings.

## 2. Networking, referrals & outreach

### Referrals
- Beat cold apply massively (one Blind funnel: ~55% referral→recruiter-call vs ~10% cold; specific number is one person = UNVERIFIED, but the **direction is high-confidence genuine**).
- **Critical for you:** a referral gets the resume *seen*; it does **not** clear a sponsorship filter. Spend referral energy only at confirmed sponsors.

### Cold outreach — what works
- **Direct "please refer me" DMs to strangers = NOT WORKING** (people won't vouch for someone they can't assess). *(Genuine — Blind referrers.)*
- **What works (MIXED→WORKING):** a shared tie (alumni/mutual connection), targeting the **hiring manager** (not a generalist recruiter), and asking for **advice / a 15-min conversation** rather than a referral — let the referral emerge. At sub-50-person startups, message the **founder/CEO**; at 200+, the role's manager/director.
- **Timing heuristic:** apply first, then send a short (75–125 word, phone-readable) note referencing one specific company/team signal + one relevant quantified achievement.
- **Vendor stats to ignore:** "17% LinkedIn reply rate / 3-touch = 93% of replies" and "70% screen / 20% offer" both come from outreach-automation SaaS / template sellers measuring their *own* funnels. **UNVERIFIED.**
- **Email-finder tools** (Hunter.io, Apollo) technically work but have zero neutral job-seeker outcome data and repeat the unsourced "85% of jobs via networking" folklore. **UNVERIFIED / selling.**

### Alumni & communities (highest-yield warm channel)
- **Alumni / shared-school ties** are the common thread in nearly every genuine "I got a response" story. Mine alumni **who already work at known sponsors**. STEM OPT's 36-month runway is a legit talking point with them. *(Genuine, WORKING, high confidence.)*
- **Real practitioner communities** (warm intros happen here): **MLOps Community Slack** (~27k members), **DataTalks.Club Slack** (data eng/ML/MLOps + job channels), Kaggle, local meetups, open-source projects. Contribute before asking. The "Discord has *replaced* LinkedIn" narrative is **overstated/selling.**

### Recruiter inbound
- **"Open to Work": WORKING with nuance** — turn on the **recruiter-visible** setting; **skip the public green banner** if currently employed (a hiring manager may question it). For an actively-searching OPT student the banner downside is minimal. *(Genuine — recruiter + Blind insiders.)*
- Put your **actual target job title** in the headline (NOT "Open to Work" / "Recent Graduate" — those don't surface in recruiter keyword searches). Load the **Skills** section with 5–10 exact-match keywords. *(Coaching-sourced but mechanically sound.)*
- **Open source → recruiter inbound: MIXED.** Genuine HN reports of insta-offers — but only for **substantial** contributions to projects companies actually *use*. A dabbling GitHub won't do it.
- **"Build in public" on LinkedIn/X: UNVERIFIED / overhyped** — heavily pushed by ghostwriters and branding coaches who profit from you posting; real neutral "I posted and got interviews" stories were thin. Plausible tail benefit, low reliability.

## 3. What actually gets interviews, per role

> Cross-cutting: **referrals are the dominant interview-*generator* across all five roles.** The items below are what makes you *convert* once seen, and what generates inbound. Certs/Kaggle/LeetCode rarely *generate* interviews directly.

### AI Engineer / SWE-AI / ML Engineer
- **WORKING (high confidence):** 1–2 **deployed** LLM apps that **lead with an evaluation harness** — error handling, cost/latency targets, PII/security handling, a tradeoff writeup + short video walkthrough. YC startups literally flag "candidate doesn't start with evals" as a red flag. Be ready for a 45–90 min **defense/walkthrough** round. *(Genuine — Alexey Grigorev AI Engineering Field Guide.)*
- **Predictable take-home archetypes:** ~40% RAG, ~30% agentic/tool-calling, ~15% doc processing, ~10% LLM-as-judge eval. Prep a strong template across these.
- **OVERSATURATED / NOT a differentiator:** plain "ChatGPT wrapper" / basic RAG chatbot (~70% of candidates have one); fine-tuning-for-its-own-sake for *application* AI roles.
- **Bar differences:** AI Engineer = API integration + RAG/agents + inference optimization + eval + shipping. ML Engineer = **MLOps depth** (train→serve→monitor→retrain; MLflow/K8s/SageMaker/Vertex; vLLM/TensorRT) as baseline. SWE-AI = strong SWE (DSA + system design) who builds AI features.

### Data Engineer
- **WORKING:** one **end-to-end pipeline** (ingest → dbt/Snowflake → Airflow) with a README documenting **failure handling / backfills / idempotency / monitoring** — production concerns are the differentiator, not "I ran Airflow once." A **merged PR to a widely-used data tool** signals credibility no bullet can. *(Genuine r/dataengineering sentiment.)*
- **Table-stakes tooling (screens you in):** SQL, Python, data-pipeline design (~70%+ of postings); then Snowflake/Databricks/Airflow/dbt (~24–31%); Spark/Kafka for higher tiers.
- **Certs: MIXED** — more useful here than other roles (dbt Certified for analytics-engineering; AWS to pass screens/fill gaps) but still an HR filter, not an interview generator. **Cert-stacking = low signal.**
- **Saturation:** healthier market than DS, but **only ~3% of DE postings are truly entry-level** — junior-hostile, mid-level-friendly.

### Data Scientist
- **Entry-level DS is saturated/declining (high confidence, genuine).** Being absorbed/re-titled into **Applied Scientist / ML Engineer / analytics-engineering**; "pure DS is glorified data analyst" is a recurring genuine take.
- **WORKING differentiators:** strong SQL + **A/B testing / causal inference / product sense** + enough engineering to ship. Reposition toward Applied Scientist / analytics engineering.
- **Kaggle: MIXED/niche** — Grandmaster is a real signal to a *narrow* set (H2O.ai, some labs) that also want 3–5 yrs exp; generic Titanic/EDA notebooks = **noise**.

### Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) — the hot 2026 role
- **What it is:** engineer embedded with a customer to scope/build/deploy in *their* environment — "half engineer, half consultant, full owner." ~20% is the demo; ~80% is SSO, legacy ETL, compliance (SOC2/HIPAA/FedRAMP), politics.
- **How to break in (genuine, HN):** the hard-to-fill gap is people with **both** client-facing skill **and** coding — most applicants have one. A strong, genuinely client-facing generalist is close to ideal. Backgrounds that work: hands-on engineers who moved toward sales-engineering / solutions / professional services, or founding/product engineers.
- **Interview:** recruiter screen heavy on **"why FDE, not SWE?"**; a **decomposition/case round** (Palantir signature — break an ambiguous real problem into a modeled solution); coding + system design; ownership behavioral. OpenAI-style take-home = build something real on their APIs (~5h) then defend it.
- **Companies:** Palantir (originator), OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, ServiceNow, Stripe, Workato, PostHog; **AWS announced (~June 2026) a ~$1B FDE org** — biggest signal it's scaling. ("+729% YoY postings" stat is **UNVERIFIED** — from a selling Substack; direction corroborated, exact number not.)
- **Downsides (genuine):** "org smell" critique (bespoke-per-customer = weak abstractions), less-defined ladder, 20–50% travel, comp sometimes slightly below SWE (one Blind report ~$220k FDE vs $250–300k SWE; AI-lab GTM/FDE can hit ~$350k).
- **OPT caveat:** Palantir/government FDE work often needs **US citizenship / clearance** — that slice is closed to OPT. AI-lab and SaaS FDE roles are more sponsor-plausible. **Palantir's new-grad FDSE (~$135–145k, DC) explicitly lists F-1 CPT/OPT/STEM-OPT as eligible** — a concrete confirmed target.

### Cross-cutting interview-mechanics shift (WORKING trend, medium-high confidence)
- **Take-homes are losing signal to AI; companies are moving to AI-assisted live coding** (Meta/Google/Canva and others): 60–90 min, screen-shared, real repo, ambiguous problem, you use Cursor/Claude Code while the interviewer probes your **decomposition, verification, and architectural reasoning.** Practice coding *with* AI and defending choices — not silent whiteboarding.
- **Coding-platform certs** (HackerRank/CodeSignal self-earned): **NOT WORKING** as interview generators. LeetCode/DSA gates SWE onsites but doesn't generate interviews.
- **Published content / merged-PR writeups: WORKING (medium)** as an *inbound amplifier* — verifiable proof when recruiters search your name; strongest for AI Eng/MLE.

### OPT difficulty ranking (easiest → hardest to get interviews, Aug 2026; directional)
1. **AI Engineer / SWE-AI** — highest demand, wage tiers help the lottery — *if* you have deployed-project proof.
2. **Data Engineer** — healthy + good wage tiers, but junior-hostile (few entry reqs).
3. **ML Engineer** — strong demand, high MLOps bar for juniors.
4. **FDE** — fast-growing, generalist-friendly, but small counts + clearance/citizenship cuts the OPT-eligible slice.
5. **Data Scientist** — hardest at entry: most saturated, being absorbed into other titles.

**Sponsor more favorably:** DE, AI/ML Engineer, FDE (higher wage tiers → better weighted-lottery odds). Generalist entry-level DS sponsors least favorably.

## 4. F1/OPT visa game

### 2026 climate (policy facts — high confidence)
- **Sept 19, 2025:** $100k fee on new H-1B petitions (panic + some freezes).
- **Dec 2025:** wage-weighted lottery finalized, **effective Feb 27, 2026** (applies to March 2026 registration for FY2027).
- **June 8, 2026:** federal court (D. Mass.) **vacated the $100k fee**; **July 2026:** First Circuit denied reinstatement — vacatur stands (chilling effect persists).
- **July 2026:** "duration of status" eliminated — students must reapply for permission to remain.
- **LOOMING THREAT (Aug 2026):** DHS weighing a **$100k fee on OPT itself** (~419k grads/yr). Not enacted. OPT is regulatory (not statutory) → legally more vulnerable than H-1B. If enacted it would effectively kill OPT hiring. **Watch this.**
- **Student sentiment (UNVERIFIED but consistent):** tighter filter, not collapse; demand concentrated at big-immigration-dept employers; more mid-loop ghosting/rescinded offers after status disclosure.

### Working tactics (LEGAL/SAFE)
- **Target proven sponsors via DOL/USCIS disclosure data for your exact title** — highest-signal filter. Prefer employers with *continuing* approvals, not one-offs.
- **Big employers with large immigration departments** hire the most on OPT: Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Apple, Meta (259 data-engineer LCAs FY2025), ByteDance; quant/finance (Millennium, Nasdaq, JPMorgan, BofA); consulting/IT-services at volume (Deloitte, Accenture, Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, Capgemini). *(Medium confidence — LCA data + reports; pauses happen.)*
- **Sell OPT as a feature:** "STEM OPT = up to 36 months of work authorization, **zero petition/fee for the employer**, can start immediately." De-risks the hire. *(Genuine, high confidence.)*
- **Cap-exempt employers (universities, affiliated nonprofits/research, national labs): MIXED but powerful** — no lottery, file any month. Best for AI/data *research* roles; competition rising as lottery odds fall; moving to a cap-subject employer later still needs the lottery.
- **Legit IT-services/consulting path: MIXED** — real W-2 roles at named firms are fine (lower pay, project-tied, bench risk, later discounted by hiring managers). Danger is the tiny unnamed "body shops" — see scams.

### Timing playbook (LEGAL/SAFE — high confidence)
- **STEM OPT = up to 3 H-1B lottery attempts vs 1 on plain OPT.** Choose your OPT **start date** to maximize how many March registrations fall inside your 36-month window — a bad start date costs a full attempt. **Plan with your DSO before filing.**
- **File the STEM OPT extension early**; begin the whole transition **8–10 months before OPT expiration.**
- **Cap-gap:** if selected/filed on valid OPT/STEM OPT, auto-extension through **Sept 30** bridges to the Oct 1 H-1B start.
- **Unemployment tripwire:** **90 cumulative days** on initial OPT, **+60 (150 total)** on STEM OPT → exceed it and SEVIS auto-terminates (~15 days) → loss of F-1 status. **Report every employer promptly.**
- **Wage-level lever:** under the weighted lottery, pushing for **Level 2+ wage / a higher title** materially improves odds — comp negotiation is now visa strategy.

### The sponsorship knockout question — scripts (LEGAL/SAFE)
- **Golden rule:** never lie on the application (visa fraud / rescission grounds), but control **timing and framing.**
- **"Are you legally authorized to work now?"** → **Yes** (you are, on OPT). Different question from sponsorship — don't conflate.
- **"Will you now or in future require sponsorship?"** → usually honestly **Yes**, but immediately reframe in any free-text/cover letter:
  > "I'm authorized to work through [date] under F-1 STEM OPT. My current OPT requires **no petition or fee** from the company — I can start immediately and work up to 3 years. I may need sponsorship for longer-term status later."
- **Disclosure timing:** no upside to volunteering status pre-interview; **disclose before the panel/final round, never after** (post-final-round springing reads as deceptive). **Confirm your exact wording with your DSO / an immigration attorney.**

### SCAM AVOIDANCE (critical — this niche preys on internationals)
**STATUS-ENDANGERING / ILLEGAL — avoid entirely:**
- **"On-bench" fake consultancies / body-shops** that fabricate employment (fake payslips, staged worksites in a suburban house, charge you to be placed). **This is federal fraud that endangers YOUR visa.** ICE has terminated **4,700+ SEVIS records since early 2025**, raided NoVA IT-services OPT employers. Consequences: SEVIS termination, OPT/STEM denial, visa cancellation, permanent fraud mark. Red flags: residential "office"; asks you to pay to be placed / for "training"; report-us-but-do-little-real-work; won't put job details in writing.
- **"Pay for H-1B sponsorship" / upfront-fee scams** — regs **prohibit the employer from making you pay H-1B filing fees.** Anyone demanding you pay filing fees / "lawyer upfront" / "advanced placement fees" is a scam (DOJ jailed one operator who collected $450k+ across 100+ fraudulent petitions).
- **Fake experience / resume mills** — manufactured work history = misrepresentation = visa fraud, permanent-bar risk.

**GRAY / HIGH-RISK:**
- **Day-1 CPT "capstone" universities** — enrolling in low-touch programs granting CPT from day one to work full-time immediately. USCIS treats aggressive Day-1 CPT as abuse (RFEs; the 2019 Farmington sting removed 100+ students). The sites reassuring you it's "safe" (day1cpt.org, cptdog.com) are **selling enrollment.** Not automatically illegal, but real H-1B-denial/status-loss risk. **Don't use as a routine shortcut**; if ever, only with an *independent* attorney.

**LEGIT but do diligence:**
- **Reverse recruiters** — real category, ~$1,499/mo up to ~$15k; "guarantees" are usually free extensions, not refunds. **No reverse recruiter can guarantee OPT/H-1B placement** — treat any such promise as a red flag.

**Universal red-flag checklist:** you pay them any fee → walk away. Guaranteed job/visa → scam. Fake payslips / report-us-but-don't-work → status-ending fraud. Residential "office," no written job details → walk away.

### Trusted F1/OPT tools (free, authoritative)
- **USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub** (official approvals by employer/FY/location) — ground truth.
- **h1bdata.info** (DOL LCA salaries), **h1bgrader.com** (DOL+USCIS grades), **MyVisaJobs** ("Visa Sponsor Score" + by-title reports).
- **Interstride** (F-1/J-1 job board + Chrome extension overlaying H-1B data; often free via your university), **GlanceJobs** (sponsorship-checker overlay), **Handshake** ("OPT eligible" / "H1B sponsor" filters), **Simplify** (per-listing work-auth flags).
- **Verify every "sponsor list" against USCIS/DOL for your exact title.** Never pay for a "guaranteed placement."

## 5. Tools & services — worth-it vs waste vs scam

> Meta-finding: review domains are mostly affiliate funnels that trash tool A to sell tool B; in the reverse-recruiter niche, **Find My Profession's CEO admitted under oath to writing fake reviews** of his own firm and fake negatives of rivals. Discount all vendor "best of" lists and lone glowing Trustpilot scores.

### ✅ Actually worth using (mostly free)
- **GitHub job lists** (SimplifyJobs New-Grad-Positions & Summer-Internships, vanshb03, speedyapply) — fresh, PR-audited, free.
- **Hiring.cafe** — direct-from-career-page aggregator, fewest ghost jobs, visa filters.
- **Simplify autofill** — the only clearly-safe "automation" (it's autofill, you click submit → essentially no ban risk). ~70% of Workday fields, strong on Greenhouse.
- **Teal** (free tier) — best job tracker/pipeline organizer (not for AI writing; hard to cancel).
- **F1/OPT sponsorship tools** — MyVisaJobs, h1bgrader, h1bdata, Interstride, Handshake filters. Highest-signal for your case.
- **interviewing.io** — the one prep platform with a *genuine* interview/referral pipeline (do well in mocks → fast-tracked to real companies). Pricey but legit.
- **Hello Interview** (system design, mostly free) + **Pramp** (free mocks) + targeted **LeetCode**.
- **Wellfound / YC "Work at a Startup"** — real startup roles (caveat: startups less likely to sponsor).
- **Manual targeted cold outreach** to hiring managers at startups — free, mixed-but-real.

### 💸 Waste of money / avoid
- **LazyApply** — Trustpilot ~2.1–2.4, broken fields, refund-hostile; one user reported it entered the **wrong H-1B sponsorship status** → zero responses across the batch.
- **AIHawk / Auto_Jobs_Applier** — free but produces junk (a hiring manager saw literal `$VARIABLE1` placeholders), **fabricates resume data**, no verified hires, ToS risk.
- **Reverse recruiters generally** (~$1,500/mo) — "up-charged VA"; no verified tech success stories.
- **TopResume / ZipJob** — error-riddled drafts, refund-hostile.
- **ATS-score subscriptions** (Jobscan/Resume Worded paid) — scores don't predict interviews (a recruiter's 12-interview resume scored 16%); do keyword hygiene free with an LLM.
- **ReferMe/Refer.me** — paid credits reportedly undelivered, still rate-capped.
- **LinkedIn automation** (Dux-Soup, PhantomBuster, Expandi, Linked Helper) — sales tools, overkill + ban risk for a personal search.

### ⚠️ Scam / risky
- **Find My Profession** — CEO admitted fabricating reviews; social proof untrustworthy.
- **LazyApply refund practices** — unauthorized charges / ignored refunds (~25% of reviewers affected).
- **Sonara** — appears defunct (~2024); don't enter payment/personal data.
- **Rooftop Slushie / paid referrals** — ethically dubious, "same few people referring everyone"; risk paying for a worthless referral.
- **Any "guaranteed interviews via mass auto-apply"** — ~2% interview rate + ToS/fabrication risk = actively harmful to your reputation.

### Auto-apply verdict & ban rule
- **Postings-layer tools that never touch your logged-in session** (Simplify autofill, Hiring.cafe, GitHub lists) = **no ban risk.**
- **Session-driving browser bots** (LazyApply, LinkedIn Easy-Apply automators) violate LinkedIn UA §8.2; LinkedIn has cracked down since late 2025 (Easy-Apply restrictions, permanent bans, <10% appeal success). Anything marketing "human-like browsing / evade detection" is highest-risk.

## 6. Implications for the resume system (feed back into the blueprint)

1. **"ATS score" is a marketing construct** — optimize for genuine JD-relevance + clean single-column parsing, not a vendor score. (Reinforces blueprint §12's honesty caveat.)
2. **Sponsorship-history data (DOL LCA / USCIS / h1bgrader overlays) is higher-leverage for OPT users than any auto-apply feature** — surface, for each role, "does this company sponsor *this title*?" (Extends blueprint §14 from data-ingest into a first-class targeting/ranking signal.)
3. **Add a "freshness + ghost-job" signal** to job ingestion (blueprint §15): flag postings >1 month old, absent from the company career page, or reposting frequently.
4. **Add an "assisted apply" velocity layer** (Simplify-style autofill, human clicks submit) — matches blueprint §21's anti-auto-submit stance while capturing the one genuine velocity gain.
5. **Consider a "networking targeting" output:** given a scored, sponsor-verified role, surface alumni/2nd-degree contacts + a drafted 75–125-word hiring-manager note (advice-ask framing, not "refer me").
6. **Role-aware project/positioning prompts:** the system already tailors bullets; it could also *coach* — e.g., flag "your AI-Eng target wants an eval-harness-first deployed project" or "reframe toward Applied Scientist for this DS market."

### Source-integrity throughline
The durable, vendor-independent truths: **referrals at confirmed sponsors beat everything; watch the source not the aggregator; filter ghost jobs by freshness; autofill don't auto-submit; a deployed eval-first project beats certs/Kaggle/wrappers; protect visa status above all (never pay for a job or visa, never accept fake payroll).** Treat every "study," "X× more interviews" stat, and lone 5-star review in this space as advertising until proven otherwise.
